Turn a finance topic into a ready-to-produce video that sounds like a real person with a point of view — never an AI reading a template. Every output rotates a different hook and a different presentation so the channel reads human, not bot-generated. v3 adds real-data playbooks for retention, Shorts mechanics, repurposing, algorithm signals, and thumbnails.

## The Two Numbers That Matter (Algorithm Truth)

| Metric | Target | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| CTR | >5% | Whether YouTube shows the thumbnail to more people |
| Avg View Duration | >35% | Whether YouTube pushes to Suggested/Browse |

Both must be healthy. High CTR + low retention = clickbait penalty. High retention + low CTR = nobody sees it.

Money Decoded today: retention 20.7% → algorithm is NOT pushing. That's the only thing that matters this week.

## The First Rule (retention fix)

Lead with the concrete thing: a number, a result, a contradiction, a loss, a mistake. Explain *after* the hook, never before. If the first sentence could open any video, it is wrong.

For Shorts: the swipe decision happens in **1–2 seconds** — show the result in the first frame, before speaking. Do NOT start with a face talking.

For Long: the first 42 seconds are the highest drop-off window. Never open with "Hey guys, welcome back..." — kills 15–25% before content starts.

## Hook Rotation Library (anti-bot core)

Never reuse the same hook type twice in a row. Track which was used last and pick a different one.

1. **Shock stat** — "92% of people retire with less than $50K."
2. **Direct question** — "How much would $10 a day be in 30 years?"
3. **Contradiction** — "Saving money is making you poorer."
4. **Personal-frame** — "You made a money mistake today and didn't notice."
5. **Common mistake** — "Everyone gets compound interest wrong."
6. **Secret / reveal** — "Banks don't want you to know this number."
7. **Mini-story** — "A guy invested $5 a day. He just bought a house in cash."
8. **Stakes / warning** — "If you don't understand this by 2027, you'll fall behind."
9. **Comparison** — "Two people. Same salary. One retires rich."
10. **Challenge** — "Bet you can't explain where your money went last month."

## Presentation Rotation (so videos don't look identical)

Rotate the *format*, not just the hook: data-walkthrough · single-story arc · myth-vs-truth · step-by-step · before/after · "what if you" thought experiment · fast list of N things · one-question deep dive.

## Banned Patterns (delete on sight)

- "In today's rapidly evolving landscape"
- "game-changer", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary"
- "here's why this matters" unless followed immediately by something concrete
- a closing question added only to farm engagement
- fake vulnerability arcs
- biography/throat-clearing padding
- narration that reads on paper but dies on screen

## Human-Pacing Rules

- Vary sentence length. Short punch. Then a longer one that carries the idea.
- Second person ("you"). Talk to one viewer.
- One claim per video. Don't stuff facts.
- Specificity beats adjectives — "$3,400" not "a lot of money."
- Allow a real pause/beat before the payoff.
- Earn the CTA; never bolt on "like and subscribe" mid-thought.

## SHORTS PLAYBOOK (priority format for Money Decoded)

### Structural Rules

- Length: 30–60s. Format: 9:16 vertical.
- Hook window: 1–2 seconds. Show the result in the first frame.
- Silent viewing: 80% watch muted. Every key beat needs a text overlay.
  - Max 5 overlays. ≤5 words each. High contrast (white on dark or brand accent).
- CTA: last 5 seconds only.
- End for loop: open loop, cliffhanger, or ambiguous ending — viewers replay = view count multiplies (post-March-2025 reform).

### The Loop-Rate Mindset

Shorts optimize for **loop rate**, not watch %. A 30s Short with 5 loops from 100 viewers = 500 views. Design endings that make replaying natural.

### Short Output Format

```
HOOK TYPE: [which of the 10]
PRESENTATION: [which format]

[Frame 0] VISUAL HOOK — what's on screen before any words
[0–2s]    SPOKEN HOOK — the concrete thing
[2–15s]   SETUP — why it matters, fast
[15–45s]  PAYOFF — the number/story/reveal
[45–55s]  CLOSE — one line that lands
[55–60s]  LOOP TRIGGER — cliffhanger or "but there's one more thing..."

ON-SCREEN OVERLAYS (max 5, ≤5 words each):
  • [Frame 0]: "________"
  • [0–10s]:   "________"
  • [10–25s]:  "________"
  • [25–45s]:  "________"
  • [55–60s]:  "________"

TITLE OPTIONS (3 for vidIQ): ...
THUMBNAIL CONCEPT (1 line): ...
```

## LONG-FORM PLAYBOOK (after channel reaches ~1000 subs)

### The 28–42s Hook Window (proven on 150M+ minutes of data)

```
GRAB    (0:00–0:10) — Show the result or state the surprising thing
PROMISE (0:10–0:28) — What viewers will be able to do after this
STAKES  (0:28–0:42) — Why this matters / what problem it solves
```

Under 28s = not enough time to earn the stay. Over 42s = viewers leave before the content. Never open with "Hey guys, welcome back."

### Pattern Interrupts (every 60–90s)

Visual cut · Text overlay · Result reveal · Pace change · Curiosity question. Wistia 2024: every 60–90s reduces drop-off ~40%.

### Energy Rotation

Demonstration · Explanation · Problem/Solution · Result reveal · Context. No 3 consecutive states the same.

### The 25% Mark CTA

Soft CTA at 25% mark, NOT end. Most droppers leave by 50%. One CTA, ≤15s.

### Long Output Format

```
HOOK TYPE / PRESENTATION

[0–10s]   GRAB — concrete result or contradiction
[10–28s]  PROMISE — what they'll be able to do
[28–42s]  STAKES — why it matters
[42s–3m]  BODY — argument progression, one claim, proof not adjectives
          • Pattern interrupt every 60–90s
[~25%]    SOFT CTA — 15s max
[3–5m]    PAYOFF — actionable takeaways, no soft recap

TITLE OPTIONS (3 for vidIQ): ...
THUMBNAIL CONCEPT (1 line): ...
```

## THUMBNAIL PRINCIPLES (CTR fix)

Data: face in thumbnail = **+38% CTR** (vidIQ 5M dataset). Dark background beats white. 2–3 words max, bold, high contrast. Show the **result/outcome**, not a generic stock element.

Three archetypes — rotate to break the template look:

- **Type A — Result Showcase:** outcome on dark background + 2–3 words. ("$1M IN 20 YRS")
- **Type B — Before/After Split:** left = poor/boring, right = rich/transformed. Instant curiosity.
- **Type C — Bold Statement:** giant typography ("NOT A SCAM", "WRONG NUMBER").

For Money Decoded:
- Avoid the generic "shocked man pointing at money" template — screams AI-channel.
- Real-world objects (cash, charts, contracts) shot from unusual angles.
- Text readable at 320px width on a phone.

## REPURPOSING FLOW (one idea → many videos)

Math: HubSpot 2024 — repurposed content gets **3.5x reach**.

1. Pick anchor topic.
2. Extract 3–7 atomic claims/scenes.
3. Rank by sharpness, novelty, proof.
4. ONE strong idea per video.
5. Different hook + presentation each time.
6. Strip filler. Run quality gate.

### One Long → Multi-Platform Pack

| Asset | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1× Long | YouTube 8–15 min | Anchor |
| 1× Short | YouTube Shorts | The reveal moment — **post within 7 days** (28–30 day decay cliff) |
| 1× Community post | YouTube tab | Browse impressions boost |
| 1× LinkedIn | Native text+image | URL in first comment, NOT body |
| 1× X thread | 6–7 tweets | Tweet 1 = the surprising stat |
| 1× Email | List | Subject `[Benefit] in [topic]` ; <200 words |

### Shorts Sourcing Priority

1. Clip from a long-form (the reveal)
2. Standalone surprising answer
3. Before/after (15s + 5s transition + 15s)
4. Quick win <60s
5. Trend capture (trending finance topic on-niche)

## ALGORITHM CALIBRATION

- Upload cadence: **2–3x/week** = "active" channel → 4x faster sub growth (vidIQ 5M).
- Freshness: videos >2yrs lose ~40% search visibility. Money Decoded is new — every upload is fresh.
- Shorts ≠ Long: separate surfaces. A viral Short does NOT auto-push the Long.
- Session watch time: end-screens to another Money Decoded video boost session = algorithm favor. Add every Long to a playlist on upload.
- Title: keyword in first 40 chars carries most weight in Search.
- Description: first 150 chars index before "Show more" — front-load keyword + promise.
