# Demand Evidence Questions

## Core Questions to Test Demand

Use these questions when evaluating the demand dimension. Strong answers don't require all of these, but at least 2-3 should have strong responses.

### Payment Evidence
- [ ] Has anyone actually paid for this (on any chain)?
- [ ] What is the willingness-to-pay signal from user interviews?
- [ ] Are there existing competitors with paying customers?

### Market Size Signals
- [ ] What's the TAM for this category?
- [ ] Is there a growing search trend for this problem?
- [ ] Are funds flowing into this category (fundraise volume)?

### Channel Validation
- [ ] Has the community expressed interest in this category?
- [ ] Are there existing sponsor RFPs pointing at this?
- [ ] Has the problem been raised repeatedly in forums/Discord?

### Urgency Signals
- [ ] Is there a burning need or deadline driving demand?
- [ ] Are users working around the problem today?
- [ ] Is there regulatory or market timing creating urgency?

## Weak Evidence Signals

| Signal | Strength | Why |
|--------|----------|-----|
| "Everyone I talk to wants this" | Weak | Anecdotal, no payment |
| "Competitors exist" | Weak | Could be wrong market |
| "It's obvious / inevitable" | Weak | No real evidence |
| "I would use this" | Weak | Founder bias |
| Paying customers elsewhere | Strong | Cross-chain validation |
| Active fundraise in category | Strong | Investor due diligence |
| Repeat user requests | Strong | Organic demand signal |
| Sponsor RFP exists | Strong | Paid opportunity |

## Evaluation Matrix

| Evidence Type | Points | Examples |
|---------------|--------|----------|
| **Direct payment** | 3 | Users pay on Sui, EVM, or off-chain for same thing |
| **Indirect payment** | 2 | Users pay for adjacent solution |
| **Stated interest** | 1 | Repeated requests, no payment yet |
| **Founder belief** | 0 | "I think people would pay" |

**Scoring**:
- 6+ points: Strong demand
- 3-5 points: Medium demand
- 0-2 points: Weak demand

## Citation Requirements

Each demand claim needs at least one citation from:
- Public fundraise data (token or equity)
- Search trend data (Google Trends, niche tools)
- Community signals (Discord activity, forum posts)
- User interview notes (with permission)
- Payment/profitability data (if available)

**Not acceptable**:
- "founder intuition"
- "I believe"
- "general market trends"
